TIPS FOR ANSWERING INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

1. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
2. Be honest about what you don't know
3. Ask clarifying questions
4. Think out loud when solving problems
5. Provide examples from your experience
6. Show enthusiasm for learning
7. Research the company beforehand
8. Practice explaining complex concepts simply
9. Be prepared to write code on a whiteboard
10. Follow up with thoughtful questions
